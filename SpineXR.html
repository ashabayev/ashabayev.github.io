Spine XR - 2023

Software Engineer | Level Ex

Spine XR is a mixed reality spine surgery training app developed by Level Ex for the Magic Leap 2 and Meta Quest 2. It lets surgeons rehearse a procedure in a safe, gamified setting.

I was the lead software engineer on the project and was responsible for implementing the tool interactions, such as placing the awl and inserting screws into the spine. I built the game for both the Meta Quest 2 and the Magic Leap 2.
